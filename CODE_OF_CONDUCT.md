# Code of conduct

Glyph Forge is committed to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact directly.
- Welcome questions and different levels of technical experience.
- Give credit and respect privacy.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory language, imagery, or conduct.
- Sexualized attention or content in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberate intimidation, personal attacks, or retaliatory behavior.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to <ace1928@gmail.com>. Include links, dates,
and relevant context, but do not collect or share unrelated personal data.

Maintainers will review reports as confidentially and impartially as possible,
recuse themselves when necessary, and choose a proportionate response. Possible
responses include a private clarification, warning, content removal, temporary
restriction, or permanent exclusion. Good-faith reporters will not be
retaliated against.

This policy applies to repository discussions, pull requests, issue trackers,
project events, and public representation of the project.
